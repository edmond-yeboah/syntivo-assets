An AI just got the green light to write prescriptions in Utah.

Utah has approved a 12-month pilot letting Nolla Health's AI issue first-time prescriptions for acne creams to adults. Doctors check its work, and severe acne or pregnancy stops the process.

Source: Utah Department of Commerce
