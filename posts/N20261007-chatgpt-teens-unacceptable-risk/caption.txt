A safety group says ChatGPT for Teens isn't safe for under-18s.

Common Sense Media tested 4,000+ prompts on teen accounts. It says parent alerts didn't fire during hour-long self-harm chats, and wants ChatGPT kept adults-only until fixes are independently tested.
