Apple's new Macs are built around AI.
Apple announced an M6 MacBook Pro ($1,999) and iMac ($1,499), arriving November 6. Apple says the MacBook Pro runs AI prompts up to 13x faster than an M1 model, and expanded Siri AI access will come with a fee in the future.
