Google's Gemini can now do errands for you.

Google launched Gemini 4 Argon on Oct 13. A new tasks mode lets Gemini keep working while you're away, and it connects to 40+ apps like Instacart and OpenTable. For now it's for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.
